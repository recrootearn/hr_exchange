About RecrootEarn

RecrootEarn is a recruiter-to-recruiter candidate exchange platform designed to help recruiters, consultants, institutions, and hiring professionals discover talent faster.

The platform enables recruiters to upload candidate profiles, earn rewards when candidates are unlocked, and collaborate with other recruitment professionals through a secure ecosystem.

Our mission is to simplify talent sharing while creating earning opportunities for recruiters and institutions.

Key Features
  • Candidate Upload & Sharing
  • Bulk Excel Upload
  • Candidate Reviews
  • Credits & Wallet System
  • Recruiter Networking
  • Secure Candidate Discovery
