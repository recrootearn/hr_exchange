RecrootEarn Account Deletion Policy

Users can permanently delete their RecrootEarn account at any time from within the app.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Login to your RecrootEarn account.
  2. Open the Sidebar page.
  3. Tap General Info button.
  4. Tap Delete My Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion.

What data is deleted?

  • Your account information.
  • Your profile details.
  • Your uploaded data associated with the account.
  • Your job applications and activity linked to the account.

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained for a limited period if required for legal, fraud prevention, security, or regulatory compliance.

Need Help?

Email us at info@recrootearn.com if you need assistance regarding account deletion.
